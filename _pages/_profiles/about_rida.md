<p style="text-align: justify;">
My research interests span experimental and computational fluid dynamics, with a focus on understanding and characterizing complex fluid flows. As an undergraduate, I developed an algorithm based on topological techniques, specifically braid theory, to identify and characterize coherent sets of trajectories in a range of model flows. I also studied the motility of microswimmers in microfluidic systems, using three-dimensional microscopy to reconstruct and analyze their full 3D dynamics. Currently, I am working on topology optimization of turbulent flows, focusing on employing automatic differentiation and neural networks to parameterize the design field and efficiently optimize flow configurations.
